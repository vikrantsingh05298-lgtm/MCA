Study tips — per subject

Proven strategies to maximise your score in each section

📐

Mathematics (50 Qs)

  • Make a 1-page formula sheet per chapter — review it weekly
  • Solve at least 30 problems per topic before moving on
  • Calculus is 15–18 marks alone — invest the most time here
  • Always re-derive formulas rather than just memorizing them
  • Solve previous NIMCET papers topic-wise, not year-wise
  • For weak students: Set Theory → Algebra → Trig in strict order
  • Target: 38–42 correct out of 50 for top NIT cutoffs
🧩

Analytical Ability (40 Qs)

  • Practice daily — 40–45 min of timed AA is non-negotiable
  • Puzzles & seating: use diagrams, never solve in your head
  • Data Interpretation: read the question before the data
  • Syllogism: memorize the 4 standard conclusion patterns
  • Blood relations: always draw a tree diagram
  • Attempt all 40 — no negative marking in NIMCET
  • Target: 28–34 correct out of 40
💻

Computer Awareness (20 Qs)

  • Binary conversions appear every year — practice daily drills
  • Boolean algebra: master AND/OR/NOT/NAND/NOR gates
  • 2's complement is a guaranteed question — learn it cold
  • Make flashcards for hardware specs (RAM vs ROM vs Cache)
  • Internet + OS questions are mostly conceptual — easy marks
  • Revise CS in the last 30 days using only your own notes
  • Target: 14–17 correct out of 20
📖

General English (10 Qs)

  • Read 1 editorial from The Hindu or Indian Express daily
  • Learn 10 new words per day with context sentences
  • Comprehension: answer from the passage, not from memory
  • Grammar: focus on tenses, articles, subject-verb agreement
  • Technical writing questions test formal register — practise it
  • 10 Qs = easy 8–9 marks if done consistently — don't ignore
  • Target: 8–9 correct out of 10
